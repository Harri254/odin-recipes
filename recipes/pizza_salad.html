Pizza Salad

Description

This a nice food to have on table for about twice a week or a month

Ingredients

Original recipe (1X) yields 6 servings

  • 1 (8 ounce) can tomato sauce
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • ¼ cup white wine vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ⅛ teaspoon ground black pepper

Directions

Step 1

TCombine lettuce, salami, mozzarella cheese, Cheddar cheese, tomato, olives, and chives in a large bowl. Mix well to combine.

Step 2

Make dressing: Whisk together tomato sauce, oil, vinegar, sugar, oregano, salt, garlic powder, and pepper in a medium bowl.

Step 3

Pour enough dressing over lettuce mixture to coat; toss and serve.
